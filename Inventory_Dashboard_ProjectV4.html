Working Capital Savings
Cash freed by moving from a flat buffer policy to statistically-derived safety stock.
SS_baseline = avgDailyDemand × leadTimeDays × bufferPct
SS_optimized = Z × σ_demand × √(leadTimeDays)
Savings = Σ (SS_baseline − SS_optimized) × unitCost Z is the service-level factor set in Settings (e.g. 1.65 ≈ 95% service level).
